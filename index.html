Common questions

Is it really free? Yes. Logging feeds, diapers, sleep, pumping, growth, solids and notes, the history, the contraction timer, widgets, Live Activities, Siri, and CSV export and import are free forever, with no ads.

What is Muslin Plus? Optional extras: a pediatrician report (PDF), growth percentiles on the WHO Child Growth Standards, week-over-week patterns, and a next-nap estimate. It's an annual subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase. There's no free trial and no weekly plan.

Where is my data? On your iPhone. Muslin has no account and no servers, and it doesn't send your baby's data anywhere.

Moving to a new iPhone. Settings › Export all data (CSV), then on the new iPhone: Settings › Import from another app, and pick both files. Importing the same file twice won't create duplicates. Your iPhone backup also includes Muslin's data.

Can my partner use it too? Partner sharing through iCloud, with no account, is planned for a future update.

Importing from Nara or Huckleberry. Coming soon. We'll only ship it once it's tested against real exports, so nothing gets half-imported.

I deleted something by mistake. Settings › Recently Deleted keeps it for 30 days.

Restore a purchase. Settings › Plus › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel the annual plan. iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Muslin, or Settings › Plus › Manage Subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

The contraction timer. It compares your timing with the rule your provider gave you (for example 5-1-1). It never tells you what to do medically. Always follow your provider's instructions.

Is it medical advice? No. Muslin is a log for parents. Growth percentiles show where a measurement sits on the WHO standards; they aren't a diagnosis. Ask your pediatrician what your baby's numbers mean.
