Not medical advice

Muslin is a log for parents, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medical advice. The contraction timer only compares your timing with the rule your provider gave you. Growth percentiles come from the WHO Child Growth Standards and aren't a diagnosis. The next-nap estimate is computed from your own logs and is only an estimate. Always follow your healthcare provider's instructions, and call them or emergency services when you're worried.
