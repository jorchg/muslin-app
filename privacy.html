Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026. Muslin ("the app") is made by Jorge Garcia Gonzalez.

The short version

Muslin does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data. Everything you log stays on your iPhone. There are no servers and no accounts. On the App Store, Muslin's privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your logs: feeds, diapers, sleep, pumping, growth, solids, notes and contractions, plus your baby's name, birth date and, if you set it for growth charts, sex. They're stored on your iPhone, in storage the app shares only with its own widgets.
  • Reports and exports: the pediatrician report (PDF) and CSV exports are created on your iPhone and go only where you choose to send them.
  • "Type it" (off unless you turn it on) reads what you type using Apple's on-device Foundation Models. The text doesn't leave your iPhone.
  • Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and Live Activities run on your iPhone through iOS.

What we don't use

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash-reporting services and no third-party SDKs. The app contains no networking code of its own.

Apple services

Purchases of Muslin Plus are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. If you use iCloud Backup, your iPhone's backup, including Muslin's data, is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Muslin is made for parents. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any entry, export everything as CSV at any time, and delete all of Muslin's data by deleting the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions: rushcentro@gmail.com.
